<chapter name="Gate Copier"/>
The gate copier copies the settings of a gate (its triggers, actions and the connections between them) onto other gates. Right click a gate with an empty copier to copy it, then right click other gates to paste. Gates with fewer slots get as much of the settings as fit. Sneak right click to empty the copier.

It is made in an <link to="silicon/assembly_table"/>, from a wrench and an iron chipset.
