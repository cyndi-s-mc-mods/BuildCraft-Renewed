<chapter name="Volume Box"/>
<lore>
Markers can be awkward at times: they take a lot of breaking and placing to change the size of an area. The volume box is a simpler solution: each corner of the box can be moved with ease.
</lore>
<no_lore>
A volume box is a box of lasers that marks out an area, without any blocks.
</no_lore>

<chapter name="Using it"/>
Right click a block with a volume box to place a one block box against it. Then use the
<link to="core/marker_connector"/> to drag its corners where you want them. A box can be up to 64 blocks along each side.

Fillers and architect tables placed next to a volume box use its area, and lock it while they do. Each corner can hold an addon, such as the <link to="builders/filler_planner"/>.
