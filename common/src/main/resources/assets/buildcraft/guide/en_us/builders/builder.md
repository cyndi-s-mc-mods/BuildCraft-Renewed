</lore>
<no_lore>

</no_lore>

<chapter name="Building"/>
Put a written template or blueprint in the slot at the top, and the blocks it needs in the inventory. With power, the builder clears the area and builds the snapshot in front of it: where the scanned area was, relative to the architect table that wrote it.

<chapter name="Paths"/>
Place the builder next to one end of a path of <link to="core/marker_path"/>s and it builds the snapshot at every block along the path, one after another.
